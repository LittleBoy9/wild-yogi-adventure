01 The difference

It is a trek, not a trip. — a trekker, in their Google review. It is the best description of us we have read, so we are keeping it.

A trip is arranged around you. A trek asks something of you — and hands something back that you cannot buy at the end of it.

We are a small outfit out of Maheshtala, Kolkata, started in 2024 by someone who had already walked most of these trails himself. We keep the route list short on purpose. We do not run more than we can lead properly.

Most people who come to us have never trekked before. That is the point. Our job is to get a first-timer onto a ridge at 11,930 feet and back down again, safely, with the mountain still in them.
